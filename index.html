About

This was inspired by my co-worker's cooking. I was sitting in my car before work, thinking about what I wanted to do for this project, and it came to me then. My co-worker had brought a dish called Pastitsio, and it was absolutely delicious.

I CONSTANTLY think about how good that food was, so with that in mind, I created a Greek-themed restaurant to celebrate how good her cooking is.
